0.1.0

The first release, and the only one. Everything before it was staging.

- Every automatic behaviour is a switch, and every switch ships off: weather,
  time of day, places, the dynamic mix, weather variants, auto theme, learning
  from skips, play history and Bonds.
- No permission is requested at launch. Location is asked for when Places is
  switched on, storage when the Library opens, notifications on first play.
- No playlists are invented for you; a new install starts empty.
- Home is only a tab when it has a page, and the app opens on the Library
  until there is something to put there.
