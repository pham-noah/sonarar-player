0.1.1

Fixes for the player, no new screens.

- "Play next" no longer jumps the queue: promoting a track that sits above the
  playing one used to advance what the app thought was current.
- Next and Previous wrap at the end of the queue under Repeat All, and Play
  restarts the track after the queue has ended instead of doing nothing.
- Tapping the progress bar seeks and keeps following the track; before, the bar
  froze where the tap landed.
- Cancelling the sleep timer restores the volume on the slider instead of
  jumping to full. The countdown now holds while playback is paused.
- Granting storage access loads the library immediately instead of on the next
  launch, and a library refresh no longer drags playback onto another track.
- Loop points with a zero fade keep it as "no fade"; lyrics handle hh:mm:ss and
  multi-stamp lines.
