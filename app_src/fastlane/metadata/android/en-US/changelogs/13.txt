0.1.2

Queue and session control.

- CLEAR UPCOMING in the queue: drops everything after the playing track and
  keeps playing, with a count in the toast.
- UNDO when you swipe a track off the queue. It goes back in the slot it left,
  and the current track does not change.
- Deleting a playlist now offers UNDO, and it returns to its old position with
  its tracks intact.
- Stop after this track: a stop button beside the sleep timer, spent on the
  next track change and cancelled by skipping.
- The "crossfade" setting is now called FADE IN. It always faded the incoming
  track in; it never overlapped two tracks.
