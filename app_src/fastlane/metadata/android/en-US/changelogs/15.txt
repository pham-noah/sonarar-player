1.0.3

Downloads that failed said nothing. Now they say why.

- Saving into the library folder needs "All files access"; reading it does not.
  A download without that grant used to die inside yt-dlp with a Python
  permission error that never reached the screen. It is checked before the
  download starts, the message is plain, and SETTINGS opens the page.
- Diagnostics now shows the storage grant at the top, so a screenshot is enough
  to diagnose a failed write.
- A keyboard shortcut no longer crashes on a focus change that lands on a
  widget being torn down.
- 32 MB of Japanese dictionary is gone: Japanese lyrics get kana romanisation,
  kanji are shown as written. The APK is about 45 MB.
