0.1.3 (build 18)

Playlist import takes the same road as a single download.

- Importing a playlist used its own copy of the download code: it never checked
  the all-files grant and it still transcoded to m4a. Both download routes now
  share one readiness check — grant, folder, writable probe — and both save the
  audio stream as served, with no ffmpeg pass.
- A refused import now says it needs "All files access" instead of a raw
  exception.
