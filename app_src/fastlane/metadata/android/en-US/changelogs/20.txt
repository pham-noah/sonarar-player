0.2.0 (build 20)

Your own look: a photo behind the app, an accent that comes from it, and a Home
page in the order you want.

- Personalize screen: Settings -> THEME -> PERSONALIZE.
- Backdrop: pick a picture already on the phone, tap the part that matters (it
  stays centred whatever the screen shape), then blur, dim, zoom and rotate it.
  Tint it with the accent so it belongs to the skin, or leave it alone.
- Accent: keep the skin's, take the dominant colour out of the photo, or pick
  one. Text drawn on the accent is recomputed, so a pale accent gets dark text.
- Home: reorder the sections and hide the ones you never open. NOW stays at the
  top, where the header is.
- A look saves as a theme, and a theme now carries the photo and the Home order
  as well as the skin.
