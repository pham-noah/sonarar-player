Personalize is now the one place for how Sonarar looks: layout, song list, themes, backdrop, accent and Home order. Each tab can hang its own picture behind it, and art is a folder of images you can add to. The accent you pick reaches every surface, including the drawer and the back arrows. Art dissolves into the room instead of ending at a hard edge. Themes are a starting point — change anything after applying one and it stays changed. Adds an update check: when a new release is out, a bar offers it and the system installer asks to install it.
