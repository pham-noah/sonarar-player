P0-2 · The settings row: 34px hit targets, a fixed 104px value slot, three unit formats

At 320dp the label is clipped to four characters ("SLEE…") while ~90dp of the row sits empty, because the stepper reserves a hard width:104 and the buttons are 34×34 — 14dp under Android's 48dp minimum, on the control a thumb hits most.

P0-3 · One state language: OFF is louder than ON, and "selected" means four things

Today OFF is a 2px white ring (21:1) while ON is accent at 35% (2.4:1) — the app shouts the state you are not in. Meanwhile "selected" is a filled slab in the settings tabs, a filled chip in LAYOUT, an outline-only chip in COLOURS, an underline in the tab bar, and a full-bleed slab for a section header.

The skew stays on the plate and the section tick in the SONAR layout only; it is not applied to a control whose selected state changes its silhouette.
